# Publishing rules

Read this before adding anything to this folder.

## Hack The Box

Solutions may only be published for:

- **Retired** machines, challenges and Sherlocks
- **Starting Point** machines
- **Tier 0** Academy modules
- **Free** Academy courses

Publishing solutions for anything else violates HTB's Terms of Service, and HTB
explicitly reserves the right to take legal action. Sharing inside a private
team channel is permitted; public Discord servers and forums are not.

**The trap:** a machine can be *expired* — no longer counting toward seasonal
points — while still being **active**. Expiry is not retirement. Check the
official retirement status before publishing, every time.

Source: [Streaming / Writeups / Walkthrough Guidelines — HTB Help Center](https://help.hackthebox.com/en/articles/5188925-streaming-writeups-walkthrough-guidelines)

## Other platforms

- **TryHackMe** — check the room's own guidance; many rooms permit writeups, some don't.
- **VulnHub** — writeups are the norm and generally encouraged.
- **Proving Grounds / OffSec** — Play machines are generally writeable-up; Practice machines are not. Verify before posting.
- **Your own lab** — no restrictions. Self-built targets make good posts precisely because nobody else has covered them.

## Regardless of platform

- No flag values, credentials, tokens or keys in the text.
- Nothing that identifies another person's infrastructure.
- Never publish anything from paid client work, a CTF under embargo, or a system
  you do not have written authorisation to test.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-targetname.md` — e.g. `2026-09-blocky.md`. The date is when you
finished it. This keeps the folder chronological in a plain `ls` and makes the
generated index stable.
